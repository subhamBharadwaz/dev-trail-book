## The idea

The revised plan starts applications in the first week instead of waiting for the curriculum to finish. It suggests roughly 8–12 relevant applications in the launch week and then 15–20 suitable applications plus 3–5 personalized professional contacts per week, adjusted to actual matches and capacity.

## Why it matters

These are process targets, not promised offers or conversion rates. Track applications, replies, screens, rounds, follow-ups, two mocks, a timed task, and the three recurring learning gaps. Different bottlenecks call for different repairs: weak response rates are not solved only by studying another framework.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse an application count with hiring progress. Track replies and stages as well as activity.
