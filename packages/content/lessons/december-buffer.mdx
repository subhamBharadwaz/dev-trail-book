## The idea

The later weeks reserve attention for scheduled rounds, take-homes, concise technical repairs, and familiar DSA patterns. The final 25–31 interview preparation buffer keeps applications and agreed follow-ups active. The date is a target, not a guarantee of an employer decision.

## Why it matters

Prepare questions about role scope, support, location, compensation, and start date. Review written terms and clarify uncertainties before deciding. If the target passes without an offer, continue active processes into January and inspect where the search is getting stuck rather than restarting the whole curriculum.

## A practical example

```js
const nextStep = {
```

## Common mistakes

Do not treat a missed target date as evidence that every completed skill must be relearned from the beginning.

## Try it yourself

List active processes, their agreed next steps, and one concrete technical repair. Identify which optional work can be dropped this week.
