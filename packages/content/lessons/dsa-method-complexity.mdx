## The idea

Use a repeatable sequence: clarify the contract, describe a brute-force approach, identify redundant work, improve the algorithm, analyze time and space, test edges, then write working code. A serious independent attempt should come before a hint.

## Why it matters

The plan targets roughly 35–50 understood unique problems with repeated solves, adjusted to interview needs. Re-solve misses after roughly two and seven days. In an explanation, separate input size, output size, auxiliary space, recursion, and preprocessing cost.

## Common mistakes

Do not count a copied solution as an independent solve. Record the exact gap and prove the repair later.

## Try it yourself

Take one missed problem, explain the brute-force and improved solution, then schedule two closed-book re-solves.
