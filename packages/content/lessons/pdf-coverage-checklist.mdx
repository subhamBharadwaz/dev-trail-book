## The idea

Track what you can explain and build, not only what you have read. A self-paced roadmap helps identify the next useful skill without imposing a deadline.

## Check your learning

- Explain one concept without notes.
- Rebuild a worked example and test edge cases.
- Review recurring mistakes and choose one repair task.
- If you are preparing for interviews, practice a timed explanation and a project walkthrough.

## Why it matters

Reading completion, confidence and demonstrated practice are different signals. Use them together to choose your next step. Interview preparation is optional, and employer outcomes are outside a curriculum’s control.

## Interview drill

### Does reading every note make you interview-ready?

No. Demonstrate independent explanations, timed implementation, debugging, project ownership and repeated problem solving.

### What should guide next week’s study?

Review applications, interviews and practice evidence, then pick three recurring gaps with a small concrete repair action.

### Does completing the roadmap guarantee a job?

No. Employer decisions and timelines are external; continue suitable processes and improve the observed bottleneck.

## Try it yourself

Complete one technical mock and one project mock. Record actual misses and choose three repairs. Keep applying throughout.
